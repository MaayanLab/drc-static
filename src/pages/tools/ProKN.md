UniProt and CFDE partnerships for protein-centric functional genomics. We aim to foster CF data re-use through increased connectivity and data integration with the CFDE Resources, build new capabilities for bidirectional navigation from assemblies to proteins to interrogate complex biological systems and cell networks, and connect CFDE datasets, biomedical databases, text mining results with knowledge graph models to understand systems-level responses to perturbations.
